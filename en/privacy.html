Naklon Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 of September 28, 2026.

In short

Naklon collects no data about you. The screen image the effect needs never leaves your Mac's video memory: it is not written to disk and not sent anywhere. The app has no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

Screen recording

  • Why. The picture that leans back and fades as you close the lid is an image of your screen. That is why Naklon needs the Screen Recording access you grant in System Settings.
  • When. The screen is captured only while the lid is closing and for two seconds after it stops, and while the effect plays from the button. At any other time there is no capture.
  • Where. Frames go only to video memory and are redrawn with the effect right on the screen. They are not saved to files, not copied to the clipboard and not sent over the network. When the effect ends, the frames are released.
  • Revoke access at any time: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording.

What is stored on your computer

  • Settings — the start angle, blur, dimming, perspective and other effect parameters. They are kept in the app's preferences ~/Library/Preferences/com.pogosian.naklon.plist.
  • Log — service messages about the effect starting and ending, with the lid angle, go to the macOS system log like any app's. They contain no screen images.

When the app goes online

  • Update check. Once a day, unless turned off in settings, the app asks Naklon's website (iamartempn.github.io, GitHub Pages) for the latest version number. The request contains only the installed version number; like any website, GitHub sees your IP address. Without internet the check simply doesn't run.

The app makes no other network requests. It needs no internet to work.

Personal data

The developer does not receive, store or process your personal data.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on the app's website before the release it applies to.

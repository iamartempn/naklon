Features

An effect you catch from the corner of your eye.

Naklon reads the lid angle sensor several times a second and redraws the screen with Metal at its refresh rate. The effect appears on its own once the lid passes the angle you set, and goes away when you open it.

Driven by the lid

The effect starts below the angle you pick and grows as the lid closes. A diagram in the window shows where it starts and where it reaches full strength.

Live picture

Whatever is on screen keeps playing under the effect: video, a call, an animation. Or hold a single frame, which is easier on the battery.

Tuned to your view

Blur, shadow, lean and perspective are all sliders.

No lid needed

Show Effect plays a close on whatever is on screen right now.

Starts on its own

Launches with your Mac and sits quietly in the menu bar. Close the window and it stays there.

Privacy

Your screen stays on your screen.

To lean the picture back, Naklon has to see the screen, so macOS will ask for Screen Recording access. Here is what happens to that picture.

Frames are never saved

The image lives only in video memory while the effect runs: it is never written to disk, never copied to the clipboard and never sent anywhere.

Only while the lid is closing

Capture starts when the lid moves down and stops a couple of seconds after it comes to rest. At any other time there is none.

No internet needed

The app goes online only once a day to check for a new version, and you can turn that off.

No accounts, no analytics

No servers, no trackers, no sign-up. The app doesn't know who you are.

Install

Three steps to your first lean.

Download

Open Naklon.dmg and drag Naklon to your Applications folder.

Allow it to open

On first launch macOS shows a warning — click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway at the bottom.

Allow screen recording

Naklon asks for Screen Recording access. Turn it on in System Settings and relaunch the app. From then on it starts with your Mac and lives in the menu bar.

Naklon is distributed freely, straight from its author, without the App Store or Apple's paid review. So on first launch macOS asks you to confirm that you trust it. You only need to do this once.

Questions and answers

Which Mac do I need?

A MacBook with Apple Silicon running macOS 26 or later. The effect is driven by the lid angle sensor, which not every model has. If yours doesn't, Naklon tells you in its first window.

Why does it need screen recording?

The picture that leans back and fades is your screen. Without access there is nothing to lean. Frames are never saved or sent anywhere; the privacy policy has the details.

Does it get in the way?

No. It only appears below the start angle, and clicks pass straight through to the apps underneath. One switch in the menu bar turns it off.

What about an external display?

The effect shows only on the built-in screen. With the lid closed and the Mac running on an external display, there is no effect.

What happens when the lid shuts completely?

The Mac sleeps as usual. After waking, Naklon starts watching the lid again.

What is it based on?

The effect engine is based on the open-source Mac Duo by Makito (Apache 2.0). Naklon is a version of it with a new interface, English and Russian settings and a lid diagram in the window.

How do I quit it completely?

Closing the window or pressing ⌘Q sends Naklon to the menu bar. To quit for real, use Quit in the icon's panel; launch at login can be turned off in settings.

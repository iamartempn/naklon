Mac Duo

Naklon's effect engine is based on Mac Duo: reading the lid angle sensor, the rule for starting and ending the effect, the perspective computation, the blur and dimming shader, Metal rendering and ScreenCaptureKit screen capture.

What was changed

The engine files were carried over with changes, and each is marked in its header. Comments were translated into Russian; the geometry, the effect rule and the spring were moved into a separate module and covered by tests; window state moved to Observation; the interface, menu bar, settings, localization, packaging and documents were written anew.
